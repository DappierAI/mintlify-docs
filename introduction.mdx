Dappier powers conversational AI for the open web. Publishers use it to launch Ask AI experiences trained on their own content and monetize them with advertising. Advertisers use it to run Brand Agents inside AI conversations. Developers and AI agents use it to access real-time, rights-cleared data through APIs and MCP.

## For publishers

Ask AI is an AI agent trained on your articles, feeds, and files. It answers reader questions, cites and links to your pages, and recommends related content. You control its instructions, branding, and where it appears.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your AI agent">
    Sync your content from RSS feeds, your site URL, or file uploads, then set the agent's instructions and persona. See [Launch your own AI Mode](/launch-your-ai-mode) and [Configure your AI agent](/configure-ai-agent).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy Ask AI">
    Embed the widget on your pages, turn on dockable mode, or host a full AI Mode page on your own subdomain. See [Configure Ask AI](/configure-ask-ai), [Embed the widget](/embed-widgets), and the [WordPress plugin](/wordpress).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monetize">
    Enable ads in your agent's ad settings, and list your content model in the marketplace to earn when AI agents query it. See [Monetize your content](/publish-and-monetize).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Measure">
    Pull engagement, conversation logs, and session intelligence with the [Ask AI Analytics API](/api-reference/endpoint/ask-ai-analytics).
  </Step>
</Steps>
